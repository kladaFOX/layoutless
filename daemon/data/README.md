# Word lists

`en.txt.gz` and `ru.txt.gz` are derived from
[hermitdave/FrequencyWords](https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords)
(OpenSubtitles 2018 frequency lists) by Hermit Dave, licensed under
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

Changes: kept words made of letters only, folded `ё` into `е`, dropped words
seen fewer than 10 times. Rebuild with `scripts/build-wordlists.py`.

These derived lists are distributed under the same license, CC BY-SA 4.0.
The rest of layoutless is MIT.
